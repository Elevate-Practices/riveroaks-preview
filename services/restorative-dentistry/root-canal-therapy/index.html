Root Canal Therapy in River Oaks, TX

When the inner tissue of a tooth becomes infected or inflamed, root canal therapy offers a trusted solution for preserving your natural tooth and eliminating pain. At River Oaks Family Dental, we provide precise and comfortable root canal treatments using the latest dental technology—all designed to restore your oral health while keeping your smile intact.

Saving Natural Teeth with Comfortable, Modern Care

Our dentist has advanced training in restorative dentistry, and takes a conservative, patient-first approach to care. If you're experiencing tooth pain, sensitivity, or swelling, timely root canal therapy may be the answer.

What Is Root Canal Therapy?

A root canal is a procedure that treats infections inside the tooth. The pulp—the soft tissue containing nerves and blood vessels—can become damaged due to deep decay, trauma, cracks, or repeated dental work. Left untreated, this can lead to severe pain, abscesses, and tooth loss.

Root canal therapy involves:

  1. Removing the infected pulp
  2. Cleaning and disinfecting the root canals
  3. Sealing the interior with a biocompatible material
  4. Placing a permanent dental crown to protect and restore the tooth

This treatment eliminates infection while allowing the natural tooth structure to remain in place.

Signs You May Need a Root Canal

If you notice any of the following symptoms, it's important to schedule an evaluation:

  • Persistent toothache or throbbing pain
  • Prolonged sensitivity to heat or cold
  • Darkening or discoloration of the tooth
  • Swelling or tenderness in the gums
  • A bump or pimple on the gum near the affected tooth

These symptoms may also be associated with other dental issues, so it’s essential to have a thorough dental exam with digital imaging.

Advanced Technology for Patient Comfort

Root canal therapy at River Oaks Family Dental is not what it used to be. Our practice utilizes digital X-rays and intra-oral cameras to improve accuracy and minimize discomfort. We prioritize a calm, stress-free environment, and our team is experienced in managing dental anxiety with compassion.

If extensive decay is detected, we may recommend this treatment as part of a complete tooth restoration plan.

Root Canal Aftercare

Post-treatment, mild soreness is normal and usually managed with over-the-counter pain relief. Patients are encouraged to:

  • Avoid chewing on the treated side until the permanent crown is placed
  • Maintain regular brushing and flossing habits
  • Attend follow-up appointments to ensure proper healing

For added protection, we may recommend a custom mouth guard for those who grind their teeth.

Root Canal Therapy

Root Canal Therapy, answered.

Does a root canal hurt?

Modern root canals are no more uncomfortable than getting a dental filling. We use local anesthesia and advanced tools to ensure a pain-free experience during the procedure. Post-treatment soreness is typically mild and temporary.

How long does the procedure take?

Most root canal procedures can be completed in one to two visits, depending on the complexity of the tooth and the extent of the infection.

Is it better to extract the tooth instead?

Saving your natural tooth is almost always the best option when possible. A root canal preserves the structure of your bite and prevents the need for more extensive tooth replacement, like dental bridges or implants.

Can a root canal fail?

While root canal therapy has a high success rate, reinfection can occur. This is why follow-up care and proper oral hygiene practices are essential.

Will I need a crown after my root canal?

Yes, most back teeth require a crown to protect them from future damage. Crowns strengthen the tooth and restore full function.
