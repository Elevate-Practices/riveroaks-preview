Emergency Dentist in River Oaks, TX

From there, we explain your findings clearly and recommend treatment based on your specific condition.

When Should You Call an Emergency Dentist?

You should contact our River Oaks dental office as soon as possible if you are experiencing dental pain, swelling, injury, or damage that cannot wait for a routine visit.

Common reasons to call an emergency dentist include:

  • Severe toothache or persistent oral pain
  • Cracked, chipped, or broken tooth
  • Lost dental crown or filling
  • Knocked-out permanent tooth
  • Loose permanent tooth after trauma
  • Gum swelling, facial swelling, or suspected abscess
  • Bleeding that does not stop after an oral injury
  • Pain or damage involving dentures, partials, or orthodontic appliances
  • Pain when biting or chewing
  • Dental infection symptoms such as pressure, drainage, or sensitivity

If you are unsure whether your situation is urgent, call (817) 738-4141. Our team can help you determine whether you should be seen as soon as possible or scheduled for a routine dental visit.

What to Expect During an Emergency Dental Visit

Emergency dental visits are designed to identify the immediate problem and provide the most appropriate next step for relief, stabilization, or treatment.

During your visit, our team may complete the following steps:

  1. Urgent assessment
    We begin by discussing your symptoms, how long the issue has been present, and whether there was an injury or recent dental treatment.
  2. Diagnostic evaluation
    Digital imaging, visual examination, and intra-oral camera images may be used to evaluate the tooth, gums, bite, restorations, or surrounding bone.
  3. Pain and infection management
    If you are in pain, we focus on identifying the cause and recommending appropriate care. If infection is suspected, treatment options will be explained based on your diagnosis.
  4. Treatment recommendation
    Depending on your condition, treatment may involve a filling, crown, root canal therapy, extraction, bite adjustment, repair of a restoration, or referral if specialized care is needed.
  5. Follow-up planning
    Some emergency visits can be completed the same day. Others may require a follow-up appointment for definitive restorative or cosmetic care.

Emergency Dental Treatments We May Recommend

Your treatment depends on the cause and severity of your dental emergency. After an exam, Dr. Lugo will explain your options and answer your questions before treatment begins.

Emergency dental care may include:

  • Tooth-colored fillings for cavities, chips, or small areas of tooth damage
  • Dental Crowns for teeth with larger fractures or weakened structure
  • Root Canal Therapy when infection or nerve inflammation may be involved
  • Tooth Extractions when a tooth cannot be predictably restored
  • Repair or replacement recommendations for lost crowns, fillings, bridges, dentures, or partials
  • Evaluation of orthodontic appliance irritation or damage

Follow-up restorative care through our Restorative Dentistry services when needed

Dental Pain, Swelling, or Infection

Dental pain can come from several causes, including decay, cracked teeth, gum infection, trauma, bite pressure, or inflammation inside the tooth. Swelling around the gums, jaw, or face may indicate infection and should be evaluated promptly.

If you live in River Oaks, Eastgate, Ridgmar, Crestwood, or Fort Worth and notice worsening swelling, severe pain, fever, difficulty swallowing, or difficulty breathing, seek urgent medical attention right away. Dental infections can become serious, and the right setting for care depends on your symptoms.

Broken Tooth, Lost Crown, or Damaged Filling

A broken tooth, missing crown, or damaged filling may expose sensitive tooth structure and increase the risk of further damage. Even if the tooth does not hurt, it is best to contact our River Oaks dental office for guidance.

Until your visit:

  • Avoid chewing on the affected side
  • Keep the area clean
  • Save any broken pieces or lost restorations if possible
  • Avoid using household glue or non-dental adhesives
  • Call (817) 738-4141 for scheduling guidance

Knocked-Out Tooth

A knocked-out permanent tooth requires prompt attention. Call our office immediately if a permanent tooth has been completely displaced.

While arranging care:

  • Handle the tooth by the crown, not the root
  • Gently rinse visible debris without scrubbing
  • Try to place the tooth back in the socket if you can do so safely
  • If that is not possible, keep it moist in milk or saliva
  • Seek dental care as quickly as possible

Primary baby teeth should not usually be replanted, but children with dental trauma should still be evaluated. Our office also provides Pediatric Dentistry for children in River Oaks and nearby communities.
