Pediatric Dentist in River Oaks, TX

Finding a trusted pediatric dentist is essential for your child’s long-term oral health. At River Oaks Family Dental, we create a calm, welcoming environment where kids feel safe and cared for. Led by Dr. Andrea Lugo, our experienced team delivers preventive and therapeutic treatments tailored to children’s unique dental needs.

Compassionate Dental Care for Children of All Ages

Our approach to children’s dentistry focuses on education, early intervention, and positive dental experiences. Whether your child needs a routine checkup or treatment for cavities, our River Oaks office is equipped with the latest tools—including digital X-rays and intra-oral cameras—to provide gentle and thorough care.

We welcome families from River Oaks, Ridgmar, Crestwood, and Eastgate, offering age-appropriate care that grows with your child from their first tooth through their teenage years.

What to Expect from Our Kid’s Dentist

Children’s oral health needs are different from those of adults. As your local kid’s dentist, we offer services that support healthy development and establish lifelong dental habits.

Our pediatric dentistry services include:

  • Routine dental exams and cleanings
  • Fluoride treatments to strengthen enamel
  • Protective dental sealants for cavity prevention
  • Early cavity detection and tooth-colored fillings
  • Guidance on brushing, flossing, and diet
  • Orthodontic evaluations and monitoring
  • Mouth guards for young athletes
  • Emergency care for dental injuries

From their first dental visit to their final baby tooth, our team works closely with families to ensure every child receives supportive, age-specific care.

When Should My Child See a Pediatric Dentist?

We recommend scheduling your child’s first dental appointment by their first birthday or when their first tooth erupts—whichever comes first. Regular checkups every six months help prevent cavities, monitor development, and build comfort with the dental setting.

Bring your child in sooner if they experience any of the following:

  • Toothache or visible decay
  • Persistent bad breath
  • Difficulty chewing or speaking
  • Injury to teeth, lips, or jaw
  • Overcrowded or misaligned teeth
  • Grinding or clenching habits

If your child needs urgent attention, our team also provides same-day emergency dental care for kids.

Creating Positive Dental Experiences

Our goal is to make every visit to the dentist a positive one. We take time to explain procedures in child-friendly terms, use gentle techniques, and create a warm, engaging space that makes children feel comfortable.

Dr. Lugo and the team also offer parents helpful tips and education to make home care easier and more effective. You can find helpful resources on our patient info page, including FAQs and financial options.

Pediatric Dentistry with Long-Term Benefits

Children who visit the dentist regularly are more likely to maintain healthy habits as adults. Early oral care can prevent serious issues like gum disease, misalignment, and premature tooth loss.

By choosing River Oaks Family Dental as your child’s dental home, you’re giving them access to:

  • A friendly, experienced children’s dentist
  • Preventive care that supports healthy development
  • Age-appropriate treatment using advanced diagnostic tools
  • Supportive education for parents and kids
  • A clean, modern facility in a convenient River Oaks location

Pediatric Dentistry

Pediatric Dentistry, answered.

When should my child first visit the dentist?

We recommend a visit by age one or within six months of the first tooth appearing. Early visits help prevent problems and ease dental anxiety.

How often should children see the dentist?

Children should visit the dentist every six months for checkups and cleanings, unless advised otherwise due to specific concerns.

What happens during a pediatric dental appointment?

Appointments typically include a gentle cleaning, exam, and preventive treatments like fluoride or sealants. The dentist may also review oral hygiene tips with you and your child.

Do baby teeth really matter if they fall out anyway?

Yes. Healthy baby teeth are essential for chewing, speech, and holding space for adult teeth. Neglecting them can lead to future dental problems.

What if my child is afraid of the dentist?

We’re experienced in working with nervous children. Our team uses a calm, friendly approach to help reduce anxiety and build trust over time.

Can I stay with my child during the appointment?

Absolutely. We encourage parents to stay with their child if that helps them feel more at ease during their visit.
