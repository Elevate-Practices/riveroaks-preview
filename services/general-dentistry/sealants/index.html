Dental Sealants in River Oaks, TX

At River Oaks Family Dental, we believe the best dental care starts with prevention. Dental sealants are one of the most effective and affordable ways to protect teeth—especially molars—from developing cavities. Whether you're a parent looking to safeguard your child's oral health or an adult prone to decay, sealants offer long-term defense by shielding the grooves of the teeth where bacteria like to settle.

What Are Dental Sealants?

Dental sealants are thin, protective coatings applied to the chewing surfaces of the back teeth. These areas often have deep grooves and pits that are difficult to clean with a toothbrush alone. Sealants “seal out” plaque and food particles, significantly lowering the risk of decay.

Our team uses modern dental materials that are safe, tooth-colored, and durable—blending seamlessly with your natural teeth.

Who Should Get Dental Sealants?

Sealants are most commonly recommended for:

  • Children and teens whose permanent molars are just coming in
  • Adults without cavities or fillings in their molars
  • Patients at higher risk of tooth decay

During your routine dental exam and cleaning, our dentist may recommend sealants as part of your overall preventive care plan.

The Sealant Application Process

Applying dental sealants is a quick and painless procedure that can be completed during a standard visit:

  1. The teeth are cleaned thoroughly.
  2. A mild solution is applied to help the sealant bond to the tooth surface.
  3. The sealant is painted onto the grooves of the molar and cured with a special light.

Once in place, sealants can protect teeth for several years, with regular check-ups ensuring they remain intact.

Dental Sealants

Dental Sealants, answered.

Are sealants only for kids?

No. While children are the most common candidates, adults can benefit from sealants too—especially if they have deep grooves or a history of cavities.

Do sealants hurt?

Not at all. The process is quick, comfortable, and non-invasive.

How long do dental sealants last?

With proper care and regular dental visits, sealants can last up to 10 years.

Can sealants be applied over cavities?

Sealants are best for cavity-free teeth. However, in some cases, they can be used to slow early decay as advised by your dentist.

Will sealants replace fluoride treatments?

No. Sealants and fluoride treatments work together to prevent decay and protect different parts of the tooth.
