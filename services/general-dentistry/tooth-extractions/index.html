Tooth Extractions in River Oaks, TX

At River Oaks Family Dental, our first priority is always to preserve your natural teeth. However, there are cases when removing a tooth becomes the best option for your long-term oral health. Whether due to extensive decay, advanced gum disease, trauma, or preparation for orthodontic treatment, our team performs gentle, precise tooth extractions using the latest dental technology and patient-focused care.

Safe, Stress-Free Tooth Removal When Necessary

Our dentist leads our team with a conservative, compassionate approach. She will carefully evaluate your situation and discuss all treatment options, including alternatives and next steps after extraction.

When Is a Tooth Extraction Recommended?

Tooth removal may be advised in several scenarios, such as:

  • Severe tooth decay that cannot be restored with a filling or crown
  • Irreparable tooth fractures below the gum line
  • Infection that is resistant to root canal therapy
  • Advanced periodontal disease leading to loosening of the tooth
  • Impacted teeth, such as wisdom teeth
  • Tooth crowding prior to orthodontic treatment

We also offer same-day emergency appointments for urgent extraction needs.

Our Approach to Tooth Removal

To ensure your comfort and safety, every extraction at River Oaks Family Dental includes:

  1. A thorough clinical exam and digital imaging using digital X-rays
  2. A customized treatment plan, including sedation or numbing as needed
  3. Gentle, precise extraction techniques to minimize trauma
  4. Post-operative instructions to promote fast healing

We also use intra-oral cameras for better visibility and patient education throughout the process.

Replacing a Tooth After Extraction

If a permanent tooth is removed, we will help you explore the best options to restore both function and appearance. Our tooth replacement solutions include:

  • Dental implants and crowns
  • Fixed dental bridges
  • Full and partial dentures

Our dentist will guide you through the pros and cons of each option to ensure a seamless transition after your extraction.

Tooth Extractions

Tooth Extractions, answered.

Will the extraction procedure hurt?

No. We use local anesthesia to ensure the area is completely numb before removing the tooth. You may feel slight pressure but no pain during the procedure.

How long is recovery after a tooth extraction?

Initial healing typically takes a few days to a week. You’ll need to avoid strenuous activity, smoking, and drinking through a straw. Our dentist will provide specific aftercare instructions.

Do I need to replace the tooth immediately?

That depends on the location and your overall oral health. Teeth that affect your bite or appearance should be replaced promptly using implants or bridges. We’ll discuss your best options at your follow-up.

What should I eat after the procedure?

Stick to soft foods like soups, yogurt, and mashed potatoes for the first few days. Avoid crunchy, hot, or spicy foods that can irritate the site.

What are the signs of a complication?

Pain that worsens after 3 days, a bad taste, or visible swelling could indicate a dry socket or infection. If you experience any of these symptoms, contact us right away for care.
