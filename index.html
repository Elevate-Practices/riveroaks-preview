FAQ

Questions, answered.

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes. Kids, adults and seniors are all welcome. Request an appointment or call (817) 738-4141.

Do you take my insurance?

We accept most dental insurance plans, including PPO (out of network), indemnity and traditional plans, and we file your claims for you. We don't participate in HMO or managed-care plans. Call (817) 738-4141 and we'll check your plan.

What if I don't have insurance?

Our membership plan starts at $39 a month for kids and $49 for adults. It covers cleanings, exams, fluoride and needed X-rays, plus 15% off other treatment. See the plans.

Do you offer financing?

Yes, through CareCredit and Sunbit. We also accept cash, checks and major credit cards.

Do you see emergencies?

Yes, with same-day appointments when the schedule allows. If you have tooth pain, swelling or a broken tooth, call (817) 738-4141 as early in the day as you can.

¿Hablan español?

Sí. Our dentist and team can explain your care in English or Spanish.

What are your hours?

Monday to Thursday, 8 AM to 5 PM, and Friday, 8 AM to 1 PM.
