About · River Oaks, Texas

Locally owned. Family first.

River Oaks Family Dental is locally owned and operated. Our mission is simple: follow the Golden Rule and treat everyone like a member of our family.

Book online (817) 738-4141

Our mission

A privilege, not a chore.

We strive to create a positive, welcoming office where patients feel at ease and confident in their care. We see it as a privilege to be trusted with your dental care, and we want to help you keep smiling for years to come.

Our dentist is bilingual and can explain your treatment in English or Spanish. Our office, originally built as a family home in the 1940s, has been redesigned as a modern dental office with digital X-rays, intraoral cameras and 3D scanning.

We're also proud to sponsor a scholarship each year for a graduating senior from Castleberry ISD.
